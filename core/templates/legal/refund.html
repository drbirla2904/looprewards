Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {{ c.updated }}

This policy covers OFFLet subscription fees paid by shops. Customers do not pay OFFLet anything. Subscriptions are for digital services; see our Service Delivery Policy.

Free trial

The {{ trial }}-day trial is free. You are not charged unless you choose a plan or set up auto-renew.

Cancelling

You can cancel auto-renew at any time in the dashboard (Subscription card). You will not be charged again, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

SituationWhat we do
Charged twice for the same period, or charged but the plan did not startFull refund of the extra / failed charge.
You were charged by auto-renew after you cancelled in timeFull refund of that charge.
Yearly plan, you ask within 7 days of the first payment and have not used the service heavilyRefund of the yearly fee less a small processing charge, if any, shown at the time.
Monthly plan, or any other change of mind after the plan has startedNo refund for the period already started; the plan simply does not renew.
Account restricted or closed for breaking the TermsAny refund of OFFLet subscription fees is assessed under this policy and applicable law. Account action does not remove any non-waivable statutory rights.

How to ask

Email {{ c.email }} from your registered mobile/email with the payment date, amount and reference. We reply within 3 working days.

How long it takes

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval; your bank may take longer to show it. Payments made by UPI to us directly are refunded to the same UPI ID.

Rewards and shop purchases

Customers pay shops directly. OFFLet does not receive payment for a shop's goods or services and cannot issue a refund for that transaction. Contact the shop first about a charge, cancellation, refund or reward. If you suspect fraud or cannot resolve a serious issue, report it to our Grievance Officer; we may review the report and take action under our Terms, but a report does not guarantee recovery of money. Your rights under applicable law remain unchanged.
