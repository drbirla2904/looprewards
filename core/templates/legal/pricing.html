If a subscription ends

Your account and business dashboard are not immediately removed. Here is what customers and shop owners can expect:

WhenWhat happens
On the end dateThe public shop page pauses: new customers cannot join and new purchases cannot be submitted. The owner can still sign in, review existing submissions, and redeem rewards already earned. Customers can sign in to view their saved progress, purchase history, and reward codes.
For {{ grace }} days after the end dateYour shop remains paused, but nothing is deleted. Renew during this window to restore the same QR, customer records, progress, and rewards without a back-charge for paused time.
After that {{ grace }}-day windowYour shop stays paused while its data is retained for up to {{ retention }} months. Renew before the retention date to restore it. Existing reward expiry dates are extended for the time the shop was paused.
After the retention dateShop data is permanently deleted and cannot be restored. You can create a new shop, which will have a new QR code.
