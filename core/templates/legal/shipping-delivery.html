Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: {{ c.updated }}

OFFLet provides a digital software service. We do not sell, pack, or ship physical products, so shipping charges and physical delivery timelines do not apply.

Free trial access

Shop owners can use the {{ trial }}-day trial described on our Pricing page. The trial is a digital service and does not require a payment card.

Paid subscription activation

After a shop owner completes payment, subscription access is enabled after the payment is confirmed by our payment provider and recorded in the account. Activation is normally automatic. If your payment is successful but the plan has not updated in your dashboard, contact {{ c.email }} with your registered mobile number and payment reference. Do not share card details, passwords, or one-time passwords, and please avoid making a duplicate payment while we investigate.

If you choose a manual UPI payment option shown in your dashboard, access is enabled after we verify the submitted transaction reference. Verification may take longer than an online checkout payment.

Access period and support

Your subscription and renewal period are described on the Pricing page and in our Terms. For payment disputes, cancellations, or refund requests, see the Refund & Cancellation Policy. For general help, visit Contact us.

Shop purchases and rewards are provided by the individual shop, not shipped or fulfilled by OFFLet. Contact that shop directly about its products, services, or rewards.
