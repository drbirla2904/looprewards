Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ c.updated }}

{{ c.name }} ("we", "us") runs OFFLet, a loyalty platform that connects local shops and their customers. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Who is who

We use account and service data to operate and protect OFFLet and handle support requests. A shop is an independent business that decides how it uses customer information for its own sales, services and reward programme. When you join a shop, it can see information needed to run that programme, such as your name, mobile number and activity with that shop. Contact the shop about its own privacy practices. This policy describes OFFLet's handling of data and does not replace a shop's privacy notice.

3. Legal basis and consent

We process your data with your consent (given when you register, join a shop or tick a box) and for legitimate uses permitted by law, such as security, accounting and legal compliance. Offers from a shop are sent only if you tick "Send me offers from this shop"; you can untick it at any time.

4. Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We share only what is reasonably needed with: (a) the shop you join (your name, mobile number, purchase history with that shop and, when you submit a purchase, your optional profile photo for verification); (b) Razorpay, for subscription payments; (c) our SMS/WhatsApp provider, for OTPs and alerts; (d) our email provider, only to deliver recovery-email verification and password-reset codes; (e) our hosting provider and, if used, a security/CDN provider; (f) an error-monitoring service, configured not to send personal data; (g) authorities where the law requires. When reviewing a complaint, we may share relevant details with the shop or other people involved to seek clarification, protect users or assess the report. We limit such sharing to what is reasonably needed and required or permitted by law. Agents never see customer data or passwords.

5. How long we keep data

  • Customer accounts: until you delete your account.
  • Shop data (customers, entries, rewards): while the shop's subscription is active, during the {{ grace }}-day pause period after it ends, and for {{ retention }} months after that, then it is permanently deleted unless the shop renews.
  • Invoices, payments and commission records: as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require.
  • Support and complaint records: for as long as reasonably needed to respond, review, protect users, establish or defend legal claims, and meet legal obligations; access is limited to people who need the information.
  • Server logs: for a short period, then rotated out.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase personal data we hold, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable law and information we must keep. In the app, "Delete my account" starts the account deletion process; shop owners' and agents' accounts may be anonymised, and billing or other records required for legal, security or dispute purposes may be retained for the applicable period. For privacy requests or complaints, write to the Grievance Officer below with enough information for us to identify and review your request. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 7 days and resolve them within 30 days where practicable; complex matters or legal requirements may take longer, and we will communicate if they do. You may also use any complaint or appeal mechanism available under applicable law.

7. Children

OFFLet is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data of children; if you believe a child has registered, tell us and we will delete the account.

8. Complaints and suspected shop fraud

If you report suspected shop fraud or misconduct, we use the information you provide and relevant platform records to assess the report and may ask you and the shop for clarification. We may take proportionate temporary steps while reviewing a serious concern and may restrict or block a shop or account after review when reasonably warranted. A report is not automatically proof of wrongdoing. We do not decide whether a shop owes you money, guarantee a refund or replace remedies available from the shop or under applicable law.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, rate limits, access controls and backups. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Browser storage

We use your browser's local storage and a service worker to keep you signed in, remember your language and let the app install on your phone. We do not use advertising trackers.
