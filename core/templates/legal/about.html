About OFFLet

OFFLet is a digital loyalty and rewards platform for local shops. We help shop owners publish reward programmes that customers can join by scanning a shop’s QR code, track their progress online, and redeem rewards offered by that shop.

How the service works

  • Shop owners create a digital shop page and reward programme, then manage customer participation and reward redemptions in their dashboard.
  • Customers can join participating shops and use OFFLet to view their progress and rewards at no charge from OFFLet.
  • Shop owners can try the service for {{ trial }} days. Paid subscriptions are available on the Pricing page.

What OFFLet does not do

OFFLet provides software; it does not sell or ship physical goods. Purchases at a shop are paid directly to that shop, and each shop is responsible for the goods, services, and rewards it offers. Shop subscription payments to OFFLet are processed using the payment methods shown in the owner’s dashboard.
